Common questions

How often should my system be serviced?

Twice a year: in spring before the heat, and in autumn before the cold. That is what our maintenance customers get, along with priority booking all year round.

Should I repair my system or replace it?

It depends on how old it is, how often it breaks down and what the repair costs. We look at it properly and give you a straight answer either way.

What is a mini split good for?

Rooms your main system does not reach: garages, workshops, additions, home offices, lake houses and guest quarters. We install Mitsubishi Electric.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. You can apply online with Wells Fargo in a few minutes.

What if my heat or AC goes out?

Emergency services are available. Call (940) 229-0715. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

What areas do you cover?

Graham, Possum Kingdom and surrounding areas, out of our base in Graham, Texas.
